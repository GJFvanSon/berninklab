About BerninkLab

Decoding the complexity of human immunity.

Our research explores the biology of immune cells in human tissues. We are particularly interested in how immune-cell identity, communication, and environmental signals shape immune responses.

By combining cellular immunology with advanced experimental approaches, we aim to better understand immune regulation in health and disease.

Research

Our research themes

We study immune-cell function across multiple biological scales, from cellular mechanisms to tissue-level interactions.

01

Immune-cell identity

Understanding how immune cells acquire and maintain specialized functional states.

02

Tissue immunology

Investigating how local tissue environments influence immune-cell behavior and adaptation.

03

Cellular communication

Exploring signaling networks that coordinate interactions between immune and tissue cells.

04

Health and disease

Translating fundamental immunological insights into a better understanding of disease mechanisms.
